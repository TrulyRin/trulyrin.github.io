Depths Saviors Bot

A guide for members and staff of our Discord community.

For members

  • Verification: use the personal link provided by the bot or staff. Sign in with the Discord account the link belongs to, read the privacy notice, then choose whether to verify. Ask staff for help if the link expires or a check appears incorrect.
  • Questions and posts: follow the pinned format and channel rules. FAQ matching and forum checks can process your posts automatically in configured channels.
  • Proof and points: submit relevant links or attachments through the server workflow. Staff review submissions and manage points.
  • Deepwoken utilities: use build previews, tryouts and the commands available to your role. Availability depends on channel and staff configuration.

For staff

The staff dashboard requires Discord sign-in and Administrator access in the main Depths Saviors server. It manages the active private modules. Role-restricted commands remain subject to their individual permissions.

Review automated moderation and verification results before relying on them. Members can ask @Rindw or @Zako_tb to review a mistake.

Data used by these features

Server Members access supports join checks and member tools. Message Content access supports post formatting, forum moderation, FAQ matching, proof detection and link previews. Selected FAQ and forum content may be sent to Hugging Face or Google Gemini for processing.

Read the Privacy Policy for browser verification data, storage, service providers and deletion requests, and the Terms of Service for community use.

Retired public features

The former public Gank Ping Bot, external server setup, alliance networks, cross-server broadcasts and public billing offers are archived. The bot is not available to install in other servers. Old setup instructions no longer apply.
