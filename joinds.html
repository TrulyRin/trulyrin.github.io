COMMUNITY & SUPPORT

Find your place in Depths Saviors.

The bot serves our main Discord server. Joining the community does not grant staff access.

Join the community

Use our Discord server invitation and follow the server rules and onboarding instructions. Membership and access to channels are managed by staff.

Open Depths Saviors in Discord

Get help or request a review

Open a support ticket in the server or contact @Rindw or @Zako_tb on Discord. Use this contact for broken verification links, moderation appeals, bot problems and abuse reports. If you cannot access server channels, contact either operator directly.

Describe the issue and include the relevant Discord user ID or message link when needed. Never send your password, login token or a private verification link in a public channel.

Privacy and deletion requests

Contact the same operators to request access to, correction of, or deletion of bot-managed records linked to your Discord user ID. Say which records or feature your request concerns. Staff may ask you to confirm account ownership.

See the retention and deletion information for current limitations.

Staff access

Authorized administrators can sign in to the staff dashboard. Public bot invitations and external-server onboarding are closed.
