Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate the Alliance and Gank Ping systems, Depths Saviors collects and stores the following data:

Discord Identifiers (IDs)

We store Guild IDs, Channel IDs (for alerts), Role IDs (for pings), and User IDs (for command authorization and statistics).

User-Generated Content

When you use /gank, we process the text details (location, server region) and any attached screenshots to redistribute them to allied servers.

Interaction Data

We track command usage frequency and reaction types (✅, ❌) to generate the /gankstats leaderboards.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Functionality: IDs are strictly used to send alerts to the correct channels and ping the correct roles.
  • Security: User IDs are used to verify permissions (e.g., ensuring only approved Allies can call ganks).
  • Analytics: Aggregated data is used to display public statistics about rescue activity and response times.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Platforms

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties. However, as this service operates alongside other platforms, your usage is also subject to their respective policies:

  • Discord: Any data sent through or displayed by the bot is subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Roblox & Deepwoken: Any account linking or gameplay coordination via our tools remains subject to Roblox's Privacy Policy. We do not bypass or exploit Roblox APIs.
  • Our Operations: All collected data is managed strictly by the bot owners (Rindw and Zako_tb) for the sole purpose of operating and securing the Depths Saviors network.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain data for as long as your Guild is part of the Alliance Network.

  • Right to Removal: If you kick the bot from your server, your configuration data remains in our database until a manual cleanup.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the complete deletion of your Guild's data or your User statistics by opening a ticket in our Support Server.

5. Verification & Browser Fingerprinting

When you verify your Discord session through our verification page, we collect data to prevent alt accounts and ban evasion:

  • A browser fingerprint hash (derived from canvas, WebGL, and browser properties)
  • Screen resolution, color depth, and touch support
  • Timezone and language preferences
  • Browser User-Agent string

This data is hashed into a non-reversible identifier. It cannot identify you personally outside of our system. This data is stored until you request erasure or your guild leaves our network.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need to report a breach, please join our Discord and contact @Zako_tb or @Rindw.
