Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The bot serves the main Depths Saviors Discord server. We process data needed for its community tools:

Discord Identifiers

User, guild, channel, role, and message IDs support permissions, moderation, points, tryouts, and verification.

Messages and Submissions

Configured channels may have message content and attachments processed for format checks, forum moderation, FAQ replies, proof submissions, and Deepwoken tools.

Dashboard Sign-In

The staff dashboard uses Discord OAuth and a session cookie to check Administrator access in the main server.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Operate member verification, moderation, and community tools.
  • Check permissions and help staff review possible abuse or mistakes.
  • Display relevant bot settings to authorized main-server administrators.

3. Service Providers

Discord handles account sign-in and server messages. Selected FAQ text may be processed by Hugging Face for similarity matching; selected forum content may be sent to Google Gemini for moderation assistance. Hosting and storage providers process data needed to run the bot and website. We do not sell member data.

4. Retention and Deletion

Operational records are kept while needed for community administration, security, and bot features. We cannot promise an automatic deletion date for every record. For access or deletion requests, contact @Rindw or @Zako_tb in the Depths Saviors server. Staff will review the request and explain any records that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

5. Verification and Browser Fingerprinting

If you choose browser verification, the page collects device and browser signals to help staff review possible alternate accounts and ban evasion:

  • Canvas or WebGL output and other browser properties
  • Screen properties, language, timezone, and user agent

A derived fingerprint is linked to your Discord account. It is not anonymous. If you decline verification, ask staff for assistance.

6. Security and Contact

Access to stored data is limited to authorized operators. We have not confirmed whether the hosting provider encrypts all stored data at rest, so we do not make that claim. Questions and requests can be sent to @Rindw or @Zako_tb in the Depths Saviors server.
