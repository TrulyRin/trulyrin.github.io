Privacy Policy

Updated September 28, 2026

Scope

The bot is operated for the main Depths Saviors Discord server. This public website provides information, the staff dashboard, and a member verification page.

Data we use

We process Discord user, guild, channel, role, and message identifiers needed for moderation, permissions, verification, points, tryouts, and support. Configured channels may have message content and attachments processed for format checks, forum moderation, FAQ replies, proof submissions, and Deepwoken tools. Staff dashboard sign-in uses Discord OAuth and a session cookie.

Browser verification

When you choose to verify, the page collects browser and device signals such as canvas or WebGL output, screen properties, language, timezone, and user agent. A derived fingerprint is linked to your Discord account to help staff review possible alternate accounts and ban evasion. It is not anonymous. If you decline verification, you can ask staff for assistance.

Service providers

Discord handles account sign-in and server messages. Selected FAQ text can be processed by Hugging Face for similarity matching, and selected forum content can be sent to Google Gemini for moderation assistance. We do not sell member data. Hosting and storage providers process data needed to run the bot and website.

Retention and deletion

Operational records are kept while needed for community administration, security, and bot features. We cannot promise an automatic deletion date for every record. To request access or deletion, contact @Rindw or @Zako_tb in the Depths Saviors server. Staff will review the request and explain any records that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

Security

Access to stored data is limited to authorized operators. We have not confirmed whether the hosting provider encrypts all stored data at rest, so we do not make that claim.
